Who I am

I am a BCIS student at Apex College focused on backend development — APIs, databases and server-side architecture — while also shipping full products when projects need end-to-end delivery.

My story

My path into development started with curiosity about how websites and apps actually work — then grew into building complete products from design through database and deployment thinking.

During my BCIS studies at Apex College I have worked on platforms for book trading, fashion e-commerce, food discovery for renters and personal fitness tracking. Each project taught me something new about UX, data modeling and shipping under deadlines.

Alongside coursework I take on freelance development work — helping clients and small teams turn ideas into working web and mobile solutions. I also mentor peers through the SMART Program, organize tech events and serve as a class representative.
