Swaad Sathi connects people living in rental housing — who often lack time to cook — with home kitchens and cloud-kitchen style providers. I worked on frontend design and development in Flutter and React Native and explored AI chatbot integration with manual training for guided assistance.

  • Mobile-first experience for renters and home kitchens

  • AI chatbot integration for guided user help
  • Solves a real lifestyle problem around daily meals
